# PA toolkit research prototype

Purpose: inspect deterministic structure on existing recorded tick-built range bars. Reuse current supply/demand events; no worker/config/order changes, ML, new feed or performance claims.

Rules v1: strict swing pivots need two bars on each side, ties rejected. Pivot only known after second right bar closes. Label consecutive highs HH/LH and lows HL/LL (equal values EH/EL). Two higher highs/lows = Uptrend, two lower highs/lows = Downtrend, mixed = Range / mixed, equal/insufficient = Transition / Unknown. This is an operational classification, not evidence of accumulation/distribution. First close beyond last confirmed unbroken swing = BOS, opposite previous break direction = Character shift. Close-only, not wick. A close breaking the current supporting low/high invalidates an Uptrend/Downtrend into Transition until new confirmed structure supports it. Reset context across UTC day or >60s bar boundary gap.

At an existing PA confirmation only: indicative entry = closed Bid bar price, stop outside zone by0.1range and at least$3 away; TP nearest confirmed swing high/low ahead of entry known at that moment. Candidate requires aligned trend and geometric RR>=3; missing target, low RR or misalignment blocks. No spread/slippage/fees or next-tick execution, no target extension. Save decision at confirmation, never recalculate old decisions using future pivots. Display latest setup explicitly historical, not currently executable. Expire at context reset. This does not replace backend strategy or its departure-extreme target.

UI: chart swing markers/structure-break markers plus existing zones; inspector regime/evidence, latest known swing levels and historical setup entry/SL/TP/RR/reason; optional structure visibility.
